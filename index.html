A little about me

Making complex ideas
easier to use.

I work at the intersection of technical writing, content strategy, and AI-assisted knowledge systems. As a Technical Writer 3 at Docusign, I create and maintain product documentation that helps customers accomplish their goals.

Alongside my professional work, I’m pursuing a PhD in Technical Communication and Rhetoric at Texas Tech University. My research interests connect the everyday work of documentation with broader questions about knowledge, technology, and customer support

What guides my work

  • Start with what the reader needs to do.
  • Give content a clear, usable structure.
  • Use AI thoughtfully, with human review and accountability.

My work connects clear customer guidance with the systems and workflows that keep it useful.

I

Web Forms documentation

I own documentation for Docusign Web Forms, with a focus on helping customers navigate the product and understand how to use it. Modernization is an opportunity to improve both the content and its organization.

Product documentation · Content modernization

II

Release notes workflows

As Scrum Master for the Release Notes Task Force, I help coordinate the work of communicating product changes. This role brings together documentation, collaboration, and process improvement.

Cross-functional collaboration · Workflow design

III

AI-assisted knowledge systems

I explore ways to use AI in documentation workflows, including content comparison, revision, and quality review. My focus is on making those workflows useful while keeping human judgment central.

03

Doctoral research

What happens when
knowledge becomes infrastructure?

My developing dissertation focus is Productizing Knowledge: The Transformation of Enterprise Documentation into Customer Support Infrastructure. I’m interested in how generative AI changes documentation and the work technical communicators do.

Questions I’m exploring

  1. How is generative AI transforming enterprise documentation into customer support infrastructure?
  2. What AI-assisted workflows do technical communicators use to productize enterprise knowledge?
